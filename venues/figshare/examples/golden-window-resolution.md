## Description（必須・記憶による）

（要旨・状態の行・開示の三段。そのまま貼る文です。要旨は PDF の要旨と同じ文です。）

In a three-player repeated game with discount factor δ, the set T_δ of pure-strategy equilibrium payoffs on one edge of the feasible set was conjectured (Figshare, DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.34419198) to have Lebesgue measure zero and a dimension in (0, 1) that is continuous, non-decreasing, and runs from 0 to 1 across the window 1/φ < δ < 2/3. We record that the set is, up to scaling, the survivor set of the greedy β-transformation, β = 1/δ, with the hole (t(β), 1), t(β) = (2 − β)/(β − 1). Measure zero, the dimension formula Σ_{n∈A_δ} δ^{ns} = 1 and the bound dim < 1 are then special cases of a theorem of Lu, Steiner and Zou; we give elementary proofs and credit them. Along this curve, where base and hole move together, we prove that the dimension is continuous and strictly increasing with limits 0 and 1; we find its exact rate at both ends, dim ∼ W(L/log φ)/L with L = log(1/(δ − 1/φ)) and 1 − dim ∼ C(2/3 − δ) with an explicit C = 11.4760…; we show that the entropy is a devil's staircase whose plateaus are exactly the parameter intervals with a fixed finite return set; and we explain why the golden ratio appears twice. We extend the reduction to exposed edges of linear dilemmas and obtain closed-form windows. Conditionally on a published continuity theorem of Lu, Steiner and Zou, we show that (β, t) ↦ dim_H K_β(t) for the hole at 0 is jointly continuous for β ∈ (1, 2], a question left open by Allaart and Kong. Finally, we give a symbolic proof of an entropy identity for lexicographic shifts on any finite alphabet, a map-level form of which is due to Oliveira, and use it for the entropy of K_β(t) for β > 2 when t is not β-rational. In a separate part, we check the claims of the three Trinity-Infinity papers one by one and treat the Series II game exhaustively where a proof was found: the exact equilibrium set on the face containing the printed example (2,4,2), the exact set of payoffs that are never sustainable, a payoff-gap bound |v_i − v_j| ≤ 2δ, and the whole equilibrium set for 1/2 ≤ δ ≤ 1/√2 (proved for all δ in that range in one direction, and by an exact computer-assisted certificate at nine values in the other); we also give the sharp convergence rates of the operator and decide its golden switching example. Tables separate what is known, what was not found in the texts read, and what is conditional.

Status. Known: measure zero, the dimension formula and dim < 1 for the golden window (special cases of Lu–Steiner–Zou, Theorem 1.2 and Proposition 2.5); the exact k-step norms of the operator and the Nash-reversion threshold 1/2 of Series I. Not found in the texts read: monotonicity, continuity and end limits along the curve, Theorems A–D, the universal window and the dilemma windows, and, in the Trinity-Infinity part, the equilibrium set on the face containing (2,4,2), the never-sustainable payoffs, the payoff-gap bound, the convergence criterion of the golden switching example and the first-order gap of the rotation threshold. Proved only at nine values of δ (exact computer-assisted certificate): that the 23-vertex polytope is the whole equilibrium set for 1/2 ≤ δ ≤ 1/√2. Conditional: Theorem E (on Lu–Steiner–Zou, Theorem 1.4; β ≤ 2 only). "Not found in the texts read" is the strongest novelty claim made. The frozen Trinity-Infinity papers are not changed; corrections to them are stated separately.
