### Summary in plain words (optional)

A three-player repeated game has a range of patience levels in which the set of stable agreements is a fractal dust. This paper shows that the dust has zero length and computes its exact size, which grows from 0 to 1 across the range. It does so by recognising a known object from the theory of number expansions, and it says which parts were already known.
